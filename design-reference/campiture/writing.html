MoralRevo­lution

A writing and conversation project that gives oxygen to ideas, institutions, and moral horizons capable of reshaping social and economic life for the better.

The project
Read on Substack ↗Apple Podcasts ↗Spotify ↗

Moral Revolution brings theology, ethics, political economy, ecology, and social justice into conversation, not as separate disciplines, but as shared resources for imagining a more just world in which humanity and the rest of creation can flourish.

Through essays and the Moral Revolution podcast, co-hosted with my friend Tony Annett, I explore questions such as these.

Question one

What do our economies ask us to value?

Question two

What forms of life do they make possible, or foreclose?

Question three

Institutions rooted in dignity, solidarity, care?

What would it mean to build institutions rooted in dignity, solidarity, care, and ecological responsibility?
