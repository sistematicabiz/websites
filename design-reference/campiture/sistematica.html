01

Research & strategic analysis

I help clients make sense of complex questions at the intersection of finance, sustainability, economic systems and real-world impacts. My work is grounded in sound evidence, careful framing, and close attention to the institutional, regulatory, and market context in which decisions are made.

The aim is not simply to produce analysis, but to clarify the choices, assumptions, risks, and opportunities that matter for long-term strategy.

02

Sustainable finance & investment insight

I provide research and advisory support to organizations seeking to connect investment practice with climate resilience, social and ecological outcomes, and durable value creation.

This can include work on sustainable and faith-aligned investment approaches, thematic and impact strategies, financial products, and the evolving materiality of environmental and social factors.

03

Thought leadership & convening

I help organizations develop research, narratives, and forums that bring complex ideas into productive public and professional conversation.

This includes reports, white papers, presentations, workshops, executive briefings, and stakeholder convenings for investment, policy, academic, and mission-driven audiences.

Why the name · two commitments
Rigor without
reduc­tionism

Systematic

Disciplined research, analytical clarity, intellectual honesty, and attention to evidence. Complex questions deserve more than fashionable language or easy conclusions.

Systems thinking

An understanding that financial, social, ecological, and political outcomes are interconnected. The most consequential challenges cannot be understood, or addressed, one variable at a time.

Italian flair

Sistematica evokes both method and sensibility: rigor without reductionism; coherence without rigidity; analysis attentive to the wider whole.

Nature-related financial risk

NatureAlpha / alphaX

Research and advisory collaboration supporting the development of asset-level approaches to nature-related financial risk. The work examines how dependencies on nature, and exposure to environmental degradation, can be more effectively identified, quantified, and incorporated into broad-market equity-index research and design.

About

Laura Segafredo, PhD

Economist, climate-finance leader, researcher, and educator.

Laura Segafredo, PhD, is an economist, climate-finance leader, researcher, and educator with more than two decades of experience spanning energy, sustainable investing, philanthropy, academia, and advisory work in the United States and Europe. She works at the intersection of finance, ethics, political economy, and ecological transition.

Her career has included senior leadership in sustainable-investment research and index investing at BlackRock, as well as work across climate and environmental organizations, academic institutions, and mission-driven ventures. She is currently an Adjunct Professor of International and Public Affairs at Columbia University’s School of International and Public Affairs.
