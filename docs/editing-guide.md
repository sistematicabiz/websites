# Editing guide

This guide explains how to update laurasegafredo.com and sistematica.biz. You edit both sites from one place, and nothing goes live until you publish it.

## Log in

1. Go to **https://laurasegafredo.com/admin/**.
2. Click **Sign In with GitHub** and approve the request the first time.

You need a GitHub account with write access to the `sistematicabiz/websites` repository.

## How publishing works

Every change starts as a draft, so you can save unfinished work safely.

1. Make your change and click **Save**. The draft is stored, but the live site does not change.
2. Open **Workflow** in the CMS to see your drafts. They move through **Draft**, **In Review** and **Ready**.
3. When it is ready, click **Publish**. The site rebuilds and the change is usually live within a minute or two.

## See a preview

After you save a draft, wait a minute or two for its preview to build. Then use the **preview** button in the entry's toolbar to open the page with your change. The preview is a temporary test address, and the live site stays the same until you publish.

## Add a Speaking & Press entry

1. Open **Speaking & Press** and click **New**.
2. Fill in the outlet, type, title and abstract.
3. Add the link text and URL if there is a link. Without a URL, the card is shown without a link.
4. Add the date if you know it.
5. Save and publish.

To add a picture, use **Image** and fill in **Image description**, a short sentence saying what the picture shows.

## Show an entry on the home page

Open the entry, turn on **Show on home**, then save and publish. It then appears in the "In conversation" block. The **Order** number sets the position on both pages: lower numbers come first.

## Edit a page

1. Open **Pages (Laura)** or **Pages (Sistematica)** and choose a page.
2. Change the text in the section you want.
3. Save and publish.

Some tips:

- Lists, such as the home page cards or research interests, can be reordered by dragging.
- In headings, `&shy;` marks where a long word may break, as in `Revo&shy;lution`. Leave it in place unless you are rewording the title.
- Putting a word between asterisks in a heading makes it italic, as in `Climate change *at* work`.
- The word "Sistematica" gets its special styling automatically.

## Edit your roles

Open **Roles**. Each role has switches for where it appears: the About page, the Finance & Climate page, and the Sistematica site.

## Replace a photo

1. Open the page or entry that uses the photo.
2. Click the image field, then choose **Replace**.
3. Upload a new picture or pick one from the library.
4. Update the **photo description**.
5. Save and publish.

Try to keep uploads under about 1 MB. A photo with a similar shape to the old one will fit the layout best.

## Change the colour theme

Open **Settings (Laura)** or **Settings (Sistematica)**, choose a **Colour theme**, then save and publish. The options are Primari, Terra, Verde and Notte. Only the colours change.

## Links, footer and search text

The same **Settings** entries hold the social links, the footer text, the default search description and the image used when a page is shared.

## The Substack list

"Latest from Substack" on Writing & Podcast updates itself once a day, so there is nothing to edit. A new post can take up to a day to appear.

## If something looks wrong

A draft can simply be deleted before you publish it. After publishing, every earlier version is kept in GitHub, so Luca can restore it.
