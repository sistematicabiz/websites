Moral Revolution is a writing and conversation project that gives oxygen to ideas, institutions, and moral horizons capable of reshaping social and economic life for the better. It brings theology, ethics, political economy, ecology, and social justice into conversation—not as separate disciplines, but as shared resources for imagining a more just world in which humanity and the rest of creation can flourish.

Through essays and the Moral Revolution podcast, co-hosted with my friend Tony Annett, I explore questions such as: What do our economies ask us to value? What forms of life do they make possible—or foreclose? And what would it mean to build institutions rooted in dignity, solidarity, care, and ecological responsibility?
